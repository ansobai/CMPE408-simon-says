Simon Says

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Simon Says collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when you use the mobile app, synced leaderboard features, and related services hosted at simonthats.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and identity information used for sign-in, such as your username and authentication identifiers provided through Clerk.
  • Gameplay and sync data, including sessions, scores, streaks, rounds reached, timestamps, and leaderboard ranking data.
  • Basic technical and security information such as request metadata, error logs, and service diagnostics needed to keep the service available and secure.

How we use information

  • To authenticate users and keep sign-in sessions working.
  • To save and sync gameplay history, player stats, and leaderboard results across devices.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app and backend services.
  • To investigate abuse, fraud, service failures, or policy violations.

How information is stored

Gameplay data and leaderboard records are stored on the Simon Says backend. Authentication is handled through Clerk. We do not sell your personal data.

Third-party services

Simon Says relies on third-party providers to operate the service:

  • Clerk for authentication and account session management.
  • Hosting, networking, and database infrastructure providers.

Data sharing

We share data only as needed to provide the app, maintain the backend, process authentication, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Data retention

Synced gameplay and account-linked leaderboard data are retained until you delete your account or until the service no longer needs the data for the purposes described above. Limited technical or security logs may be retained for a reasonable period for security, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your Simon Says account directly inside the app from Settings > Account > Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your sign-in account, synced sessions, stats, and leaderboard entry associated with the app.

Additional deletion instructions are available at /account-deletion.

Children's privacy

Simon Says is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact support@simonthats.com.
