Simon Says

Account Deletion

Updated: May 21, 2026

Simon Says supports in-app account deletion. You can permanently remove your sign-in account and synced app data directly from the mobile app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Simon Says app.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to remove.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Open the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the deletion prompt.

What is deleted

  • Your Simon Says sign-in account used through Clerk.
  • Your synced sessions and gameplay history stored for the app.
  • Your player stats and leaderboard entry associated with the app.

What may be retained

We may retain limited technical or security logs for a reasonable period when necessary for fraud prevention, abuse handling, legal compliance, or service security.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access the app and still need deletion help, contact support@simonthats.com and include enough information to identify the account, such as your username or the email tied to your sign-in provider.
